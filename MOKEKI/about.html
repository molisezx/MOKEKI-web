ABOUT MOKEKI

Stories That Move You.

A HOME FOR STORIES THAT MATTER

MOKEKI is an original streaming-platform concept inspired by the richness of African storytelling and the creative spirit of Lesotho. Our idea is to bring films, series, documentaries and educational stories together in one welcoming digital space.

From mountain communities to city streets, from laughter to learning, MOKEKI celebrates stories that connect people, preserve culture and inspire new perspectives.

Our vision: make meaningful storytelling easy to discover, explore and share.
